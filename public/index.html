Add your site

Paste your published site URL. The directory reads your api/manifest.json, did.json and signed api/profile.json, then verifies the signature with your key.

Only public profile fields are indexed. Re-submit after editing your profile, or let the scheduled refresh pick changes up.

Removing yourself

Removal needs a fresh request signed with the Ed25519 key behind your site. The removal page issues a nonce and walks you through signing it locally.

Open the removal page

How it works

  • You opt in by submitting your own site URL.
  • Ownership is proven by the signed profile on your site.
  • Edits are picked up on your next publish and refresh.
  • Deletion needs a signed request, or an admin takedown.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.
